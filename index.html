What a student actually hits

A laptop RTX with 8 GB of VRAM runs a 7B language model slowly. It cannot fine tune one. It cannot generate a 720p video clip. It cannot hold a robotics simulation with visual sensors and a policy network at the same time. It cannot serve a model to a room of twenty workshop attendees.

The student does not lack the skill. The student lacks memory bandwidth and VRAM, and those are purchasable.

03 / The Proposal

Install a GPU suite in the Smart Lab.
Operate it like infrastructure.

Not a club purchase. A transparent, logged, managed resource for approved student projects and workshops, with a real governance model attached before a single card is ordered.

Host

Smart Lab

Access

VPN then SSH

Students connect through approved Illinois Tech network controls. Access is individual, logged and revocable. Every job is attributable to a named user who signed the usage policy. There are no shared credentials and no anonymous compute.

Administration

Student admins

Executive members across five organizations handle setup, documentation, maintenance and onboarding under Smart Lab oversight. Root access travels with the office, not the person, so graduation never creates an access gap.

This gives Illinois Tech a student facing computing solution, not another club purchase for one organization to use exclusively.

Warranty and RMA

Three years, repair or replace.

NVIDIA RTX PRO workstation graphics cards, a category that includes the RTX A6000 and RTX 6000 Ada, carry three year coverage under a repair or replace policy. A failure in month four therefore costs Illinois Tech nothing.

Claims route through the board partner, typically PNY or Leadtek, or through NVIDIA support directly. Proof of purchase is required and partner review typically completes within one to three business days. The Amazon invoice is retained by the ML Club faculty advisor as owner of record, so the document does not leave with a graduating student.

This is the single strongest reason not to buy used. Used enterprise Ampere cards trade at roughly $2,600 to $3,800, and that discount is because it forfeits coverage.

Physical requirements

What the Smart Lab needs to supply.

The card side is fully specified so the constraints conversation with Professor Hajek starts from numbers rather than estimates.

Owning enables what renting cannot

No per minute anxiety. Students experiment, fail and retry, which is how learning works and exactly what metered billing punishes.

A persistent cluster is lower risk

Powering hardware up and down to control cloud spend increases maintenance burden and introduces failure modes a continuously running system does not have.

The asset persists

Cloud credits expire and leave nothing behind. A GPU still serves students in year five, and managing it is itself a substantial learning outcome.

Continuity design
  • 01Root access by office. Presidents of each affiliated organization hold root. Credentials transfer during standard executive handover and outgoing access is revoked.
  • 02Faculty independent access. The ML Club advisor retains access as owner of record, guaranteeing institutional access even if an entire executive board turns over at once.
  • 03Break coverage. Administration is remote over SSH, so July and December work identically to term time. Physical intervention falls to advisors or a named faculty group.
  • 04Trained cohort. Executive members across five organizations are being trained now, including incoming undergraduates who took executive roles specifically to carry this work.
  • 05Documentation location. Operating runbooks are kept in a standardized location in the server room so future executives inherit them without a personal handover.

Every AI company begins as somebody's unfunded experiment. The difference between a project and a startup is usually whether the founder could run the thing long enough to show it to someone. That is a hardware question before it is a business question.
